# Privacy Policy — LinkedIn Connection Helper

**Last updated:** February 28, 2026

## Data Collection

LinkedIn Connection Helper does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

## What Data Is Stored

All data is stored **locally in your browser** using Chrome's Storage API:

- Your personal info (name, university, degree) — entered by you
- Your message templates — editable by you
- Your extension preferences (e.g., debug mode on/off)

This data never leaves your browser and is never sent to any server.

## Permissions Used

| Permission                    | Purpose                                                               |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `activeTab`                   | Reads LinkedIn profile names/headlines to personalize messages        |
| `storage`                     | Saves your preferences and templates locally                          |
| `clipboardWrite`              | Copies messages to your clipboard                                     |
| Host access to `linkedin.com` | Injects the floating quick-copy panel and auto-fills connection notes |

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking.
